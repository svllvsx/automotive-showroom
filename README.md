<h1 align="center">Automotive Showroom</h1>
<p align="center">A cinematic vehicle showcase with discovery, comparison and a guided purchase journey.</p>

<p align="center"><img alt="automotive" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/automotive-172554?style=flat-square"> <img alt="showroom" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/showroom-172554?style=flat-square"> <img alt="vehicle-catalog" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/vehicle--catalog-172554?style=flat-square"> <img alt="responsive-design" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/responsive--design-172554?style=flat-square"> <img alt="interactive-experience" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/interactive--experience-172554?style=flat-square"></p>

A public showcase of a private project, presenting anonymized interfaces and key workflows. Source code and internal project materials remain private.

## Key workflows

- Discover and filter vehicles
- Explore details, views and comparisons
- Follow the purchase journey to a contact form

## Gallery

Screens are presented individually with their proportions preserved. Identifying branding, contacts and data are replaced with demo content. These previews illustrate the product and do not imply a live service is available.

Some images were anonymized with AI.
